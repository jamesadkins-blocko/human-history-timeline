# Research Rules

1. No AI-invented historical content.
2. Every included assertion must derive from an identifiable source or identifiable textual/traditional corpus.
3. Classification describes evidence/source type; it is not a truth score.
4. Preserve religious, traditional, legendary, disputed, archaeological, and historically documented claims with explicit qualification.
5. Preserve competing chronologies.
6. Never move a manuscript date onto the figure or event described by the manuscript.
7. Existence alone is insufficient for inclusion. Require prominence, historical significance, synchronization value, or major importance within a significant corpus.
8. Prefer primary sources, museums, archives, academic projects, scholarly reference works, and authoritative institutional databases.
9. Keep external IDs when available.
10. Technology is modeled as development and diffusion, not simplistic single-inventor mythology.
11. The same mathematical year must map to the same timeline coordinate in every geographic/cultural lane.
12. AI may organize, reconcile, classify, validate, and visualize. It may not fabricate historical content.
